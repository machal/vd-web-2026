# FrontKon 2026: the bits I would not miss

Next Tuesday, 6 October, hundreds of people who build the web meet at O2 Universum in Prague. The conference is called [FrontKon](https://www.frontkon.tech/en), it is one of the largest events for web developers in Czechia, and I am one of the people who started it years ago.

These days I sit in the core organising team, chair the programme committee, open the day at ten with a short Initial Commit, and moderate two panels in the afternoon.

So yes, this is an invitation. Mostly, though, I want to show you where the months went.

One practical note before we start: all Monday workshops run in English, and so do the talks from the international speakers. A good part of the Czech line-up is, unsurprisingly, in Czech.

## What is breaking in development right now {#what-is-breaking}

Agents made us faster and threw our whole craft into the air at the same time. Nobody quite knows what being a developer means today, how much code we still have to read, or what to do about software that grows faster than our ability to understand it.

I wrote those questions down in [AI restart: five questions the developer community has to answer](ai-restart.md). I do not have the answers.

Which is exactly why this year's programme looks the way it does. The article asks. FrontKon argues about it out loud.

## Two panels, where it gets loudest {#panels}

These two blocks are the heart of the conference for me. I moderate both together with Robin Pokorný, my co-host from the FrontKec podcast.

- **14:00** — [Panel Discussion: "Agent-Based Development in Practice"](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/panel-discussion-agent-based-development-in-practice). Kamil Tomšík, Petr Glaser and Sylwia Laskowska disagree about how much work you can hand to agents, which is precisely what I want from a panel — to find the line between useful delegation and losing control.
- **16:15** — [Panel Discussion: "The Present and Future of Work in IT"](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/panelova-diskuze-soucasnost-a-budoucnost-prace-v-it). Markéta Krejčí brings the HR view, Riki Fridrich the developer view, and Petr Burian, former CTO of Livesport, the manager's. Do not expect a forecast. Expect three angles on a change we are all already living through.

## Where AI shows up in the programme {#ai-track}

AI refused to stay in one box this year and leaked into the whole schedule. The ones I am most curious about:

- **Sara Polak:** [Uncovering the Future: What Archaeology and Anthropology Can Teach Us About AI, Technology, and Society](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/odhalovani-budoucnosti-pripravenost-umele-inteligence-s-pomoci-archeologie). The widest lens of the day, right after the doors close — why some societies absorb a new technology and others come apart around it.
- **Palo Otčenáš:** [Agents in CI: How Autonomous AI Cleans Up 3,000 Flags Nobody Wants to Touch](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/agents-in-ci-how-autonomous-ai-cleans-up-3-000-flags-nobody-wants-to-touch). The most concrete agentic case study on the schedule, including the finding that the hardest part was not technical.
- **Martin Krištof:** [Stop Writing Playwright Tests: Let AI Generate Them](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/prestante-psat-playwright-testy-nechte-je-generovat-ai). A live demo of an agent exploring an app, writing the tests, and repairing them once the UI moves.
- **Libor Vaněk:** [The Real Cost of AI](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/the-real-cost-of-ai). The GPU bill nobody puts on a slide. Libor runs the hardware, so he knows the number.
- **Tejas Kumar:** [Frontend After AI: The New UX](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/frontend-after-ai-the-new-ux). The closing question of the day: what are carousels and tabs for when an agent is the one using your interface.

## Seven names I want to point you to {#seven-names}

- **Riki Fridrich:** [Alzheimer – Bad for Humans, Great for Code](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/alzheimers-memory-leaks-in-java-script). Riki can take a deep JavaScript topic and make it fun. A safe bet at FrontKon.
- **Robin Pokorný:** [AI Made Every Developer a Tech Lead. Now What?](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/ai-made-every-developer-a-tech-lead-now-what). The decisions tech leads used to own have landed on everyone. Robin suggests what to borrow from staff+ engineers.
- **Jan Kryšpín:** [AI-powered Design System Development](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/how-we-develop-and-improve-user-experience-of-spirit-design-system-using-ai). Custom rules and skills for agents, design review in Figma. If you maintain a design system, this is your slot.
- **David Maixner and Martin Kocourek:** [Everyone wants a fast website. Until they have to remove something from it.](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/everyone-wants-a-fast-website-until-they-have-to-remove-something-from-it). My favourite. Performance is not Lighthouse, it is a negotiation with marketing, product and editors. Told from a real rebrand of a large gambling operator.
- **Kateřina Boušková:** [7 Stage Fuck-Ups](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/7-fuck-ups-at-the-stage). The one talk you can use at your next standup. Or at a wedding.
- **Sylwia Laskowska:** [Your Browser Is a Compute Platform (And You're Using It for Forms)](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/your-browser-is-a-compute-platform-and-youre-using-it-for-forms). WebAssembly, WebGPU, and the question of how much power we leave sitting idle in the browser.
- **Karel Navrátil:** [I Will Destroy Your Code and Probably Your Life](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/talks/i-will-destroy-your-code-and-probably-your-life). My community discovery of the last few editions. A practising lawyer who is also a frontend designer, and one of our best-rated speakers.

## Workshops with seats left {#workshops}

On Monday 5 October, the day before the conference, we run [full workshops](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/workshops). Small groups, English, € 219. Two are already sold out, four still have room:

- **Riki Fridrich, 9:00:** [Karpathy LLM wiki: long-term memory for you or your project](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/workshops/karpathy-llm-wiki-long-term-memory-for-you-or-your-project). You build a knowledge wiki you own, rather than one tied to a single model. I would take this one for myself.
- **Artem Zakharchenko, 9:00:** [React Component Testing with Vitest Browser Mode](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/workshops/react-component-testing-with-vitest-browser-mode). The author of Mock Service Worker will ruin JSDOM for you permanently, then show how components are tested in 2026.
- **Manuel Matuzović, 14:00:** [Testing Accessibility with LLMs](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/workshops/pristupnost-v-reactu-od-teorie-k-funkcnimu-kodu). Accessibility in React and Next.js from someone who has written one of the best blogs on the subject for years.
- **Kamil Tomšík, 14:00:** [Development with local LLMs: Privacy & Performance in Practice](https://www.frontkon.tech/en/events/frontkon-26/workshops/developing-with-local-ll-ms-privacy-performance-and-practice). When a local model makes sense, what hardware to buy, and where the limits are.
