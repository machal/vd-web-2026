# An invitation to WebExpo 2026: Switch Angel, Daniel Cuthbert, Mike Kus & FrontKec

[WebExpo](https://webexpo.net/), the big web conference here in Prague, lands at the end of May this year (May 27–29), and I've of course already blocked out the city's Lucerna venue in my calendar.

I went through the [program](https://webexpo.net/prague2026/program/) and picked four talks I'd go to in a parallel universe — the one where I don't bump into one of you right before the talk and get lost in conversation.

![WebExpo 2026 promo: recommended talks and the MICHALEK26 discount code](/assets/img/content/dest/machal-webexpo-2026.webp)

I also helped curate the frontend talks for FrontKon. [Our picks there](https://www.frontkon.tech/cs/blog/co-si-nenech-ujit-na-web-expo-2026) cover a broader range. Here's my shorter, personal selection.

## Four talks I'd hate to miss {#talks}

1. [**Switch Angel: Patterns for restarting the world**](https://webexpo.net/prague2026/sessions/patterns-for-restarting-the-world/)  
   The opening keynote. A well-known streamer, live coding music in Strudel, electronic pop in real time. Really looking forward to this one.

2. [**Daniel Cuthbert: From HAL to Replicants**](https://webexpo.net/prague2026/sessions/daniel-cuthbert-coming-in-2026/)  
   Agents, payments, security — a world where AI doesn't just write your commit messages but also makes decisions for you. I don't think this will be sci-fi storytelling; it'll be hard reality.

3. [**Michal Kessel Shitrit: Don't trust the bot**](https://webexpo.net/prague2026/sessions/dont-trust-the-bot-a-human-framework-for-wvaluating-ai-copy/)  
   How not to send your users straight to hell with AI-generated microcopy. UX writing with common sense — exactly what I'm [dealing with at work](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:share:7452267804730286080/) right now.

4. [**Mike Kus: Design like no-one is watching**](https://webexpo.net/prague2026/sessions/mike-kus-coming-in-2026/)  
   Design without fear of AI templates. A big name from the UX scene calling for creative instinct. Where do I sign?
