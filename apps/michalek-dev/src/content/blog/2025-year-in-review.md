# My 2025 in review: strategic, successful — and anxious

2025 was a pivot year for me — which means it was not only positive. If it had been, there would be little to learn and this post would be boring.

## A little anxiety about the world {#uzkost}

I'll start with the "anxious" part of the title. Many of us — especially if you've been around a while — live personally comfortable lives while feeling heavier about geopolitics and society. I won't dwell on it here, but honesty demands naming the frame.

## From freelancer to business owner {#podnikatelem}

I think I finally crossed from freelancer to business owner — one role among many. I still write, care about performance, co-run a company, product-manage, work with communities, and above all am a parent.

What changed in 2025 is how I think about earning. I spent [more than twenty years](https://www.vzhurudolu.cz/blog/192-volna-noha-20) freelancing; inertia is real. Running a company with a team is a different sport. That mental shift took me roughly five years.

For years I asked: *If this fails or stops being fun, I'll go back to freelancing, right?* Until late 2025, when the honest answer became: *I'd look for the next thing to build.* That surprised me.

![Evolution — freelancer to founder](https://res.cloudinary.com/vzhurudolu-cz/image/upload/v1767688556/vzhurudolu-blog/evoluce_keryv9.jpg)

## PageSpeed.ONE: consulting thrived; product needs focus {#pagespeed}

[PageSpeed.ONE](https://pagespeed.one/) — colleagues helping teams speed up sites and improve conversions — had an excellent year. With a new teammate we pushed into backend, infrastructure (Cloudflare gave us homework), and platforms like [WordPress](https://pagespeed.one/wordpress), [Shoptet](https://pagespeed.one/shoptet), and Shopify.

I spent a lot of time on sales, which surprised me by being genuinely enjoyable. We worked with dozens of new clients — Česká spořitelna among them — and deepened work with Heureka and Datart.

We also advanced our [speed monitoring SaaS](https://pagespeed.one/monitoring-plus) — development moved, but not as fast as we'd like. Agency-to-product transitions are hard; that is the main goal for 2026.

## FrontKon and Frontendisti.cz {#frontendisti}

Another mental shift: [Frontendisti.cz](https://www.frontendisti.cz/) and [FrontKon](https://www.frontkon.cz/). Keeping a volunteer-driven community and conference sustainable required new thinking. Early in the year I was close to stepping back from FrontKon organisation — until we reframed how we run it.

FrontKon in 2025 grew larger than the community itself. We have ideas for where that leads. We also launched [FrontKec](https://www.frontendisti.cz/frontkec), our podcast under the community brand, while winding down Vzhůru dolů output — as regular readers may have noticed.

Growth without slack breeds a crisis. Mid-year I hit overload: too many threads, not enough deep work, sleep slipping — familiar alarms from past career transitions. I reacted slower than I should have.

## (Not) in shape {#neforma}

Most 2025 goals went well except physical fitness. Stress, age, and maintenance-level exercise stopped being enough. Fixing that is on the 2026 list — yes, joining the January crowd.

## "2025 was great!" {#honza}

One big personal project: getting our older son into secondary school — stressful in Prague, competitive, limited places. It worked out; he landed well and grew into independence.

On New Year's morning he shouted: "2025 was great!" My wife and I exchanged a look — gym placement, good progress, pride.

Then he added: "...because I finally started drinking and smoking weed!"

So — for 2026, may you chase **your** dreams, not everyone else's.
